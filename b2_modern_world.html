STEP 05 · GRAMMAR FOCUS📝 Reported speech

🎓 How reported speech works

  • Tenses shift back one step: am/is → was, do → did, will → would, can → could.
  • say vs tell: say (that)… but tell + person + (that)… → He said he was tired. He told me he was tired.
  • Questions: use asked (if/whether) for yes/no, and normal word order (no do/does/did) → "Do you study?" → She asked if I studied.
  • Commands: told / asked + person + to + verb → "Be quiet!" → He told us to be quiet.
  • Time words shift: now → then, today → that day, tomorrow → the next day.
"I am tired." → He said (that) he WAS tired.
"Did you revise?" → She asked IF I HAD revised.

STEP 05 · GRAMMAR FOCUS📝 Present perfect: simple vs continuous

💼 Which one and why?

  • Present perfect simple have/has + past participle → finished actions, results, how many. → I have applied for five jobs.
  • Present perfect continuous have/has been + -ing → length of time, ongoing activity, how long. → I have been looking for work for months.
  • Use for + a period and since + a starting point: for two years / since 2024.
  • State verbs (be, know, have = possess) usually stay simple: I have known her since school.
RESULT / how many → present perfect SIMPLE
DURATION / how long → present perfect CONTINUOUS

STEP 05 · GRAMMAR FOCUS📝 The passive voice

🚄 Building the passive

  • Form: be (in the right tense) + past participle. The object becomes the subject.
  • Present simple: EVs are made in many countries.
  • Present continuous: A new line is being built.
  • Past simple: The road was widened last year.
  • Present perfect: Fares have been raised again.
  • Use by only if the "doer" matters: The car was designed by engineers.
We test the cars → The cars ARE TESTED (by us).
They are building a station → A station IS BEING BUILT.

STEP 05 · GRAMMAR FOCUS📝 Future forms

📱 Which future and why?

  • will + verb → predictions, opinions, instant decisions: I think it'll be amazing. I'll help you.
  • be going to + verb → plans/intentions & evidence: I'm going to upgrade my phone. The battery's going to die.
  • Present continuous → fixed arrangements with a time: I'm meeting him at 5.
  • will have + past participle (future perfect) → finished before a future point: By 2030 AI will have changed everything.
prediction/opinion → WILL · plan/evidence → GOING TO
finished before a future time → WILL HAVE + p.p.

💬 Useful phrases for any topic

Opinion: "If you ask me…" / "I'd say that…" / "Personally, I reckon…"
Agreeing/disagreeing: "That's a fair point, but…" / "I see what you mean, however…"
Giving examples: "For instance…" / "Take my own school, for example…"
Comparing then & now: "These days…, whereas in the past…" / "It's becoming more and more…"
Speculating: "It might…" / "In the future, I imagine…" / "It's likely that…"
